How to play

1. Pick a song. Choose a song and a level. New here? Choose Easy.

2. Hit the notes. Colored blocks fall down 4 lanes. When a block touches the line, press its key (D F J K) or tap its lane.

3. Be on time. Perfect timing scores the most. Misses drain your health bar, and the song ends if it empties.

4. Level up. You earn XP after every song. See your Profile and Achievements.

Want more? Import your own song, make charts in the Chart editor, try Modifiers, or watch your Replays.

Play with friends. Open Multiplayer to host or join a lobby and compete on score.

Tip: If hits feel early or late, run Calibrate in Settings. Press Esc to pause.

Calibration

Tap anywhere or press any key in time with the clicks. Use the speakers or headphones you play with.
